# Making densities (multimers/aggregates/objects) whole over PBC

> MDVWhole can be used to make broken segments over PBC whole. All you need is a coordinate and or trajectory file. It does not make any copies of the box and can handle an arbitrary amount of consecutive PBC crossings. Making things whole runs near real time on a laptop for systems including up to millions of particles.    Testing is still ongoing (beta), but functionality can be expected to give the right results.
